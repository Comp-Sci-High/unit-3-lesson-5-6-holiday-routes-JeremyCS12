Mr. Castro's Coal

A light-hearted, fictional explanation

Note: This page tells a playful, fictional tale about a character named Mr. Castro. It is not about any real person.

On the last snowy night before Christmas, Mr. Castro found a lump of coal in his stocking. Why? Because he had been a little too mischievous this year. Here are the silly and naughty things he did (all in good fun):

  1. He hid everyone's staplers around the classroom and laughed when people searched for them.
  2. He accidentally (or not-so-accidentally) re-gifted a questionable fruitcake that was older than the calendar.
  3. He sang loudly off-key during carol rehearsal and convinced the choir to try a kazoo solo.
  4. He left muddy boots in the hallway so everyone had to do an extra sweep after holiday practice.
  5. He swapped the sugar for salt in the cookie-baking contest (the judges survived, but barely!).
  6. He used the last of the hot chocolate and didn't make a fresh pot for the rest of the crew.

Because of these cheeky stunts, the holiday spirits decided a little coal would help him reflect and maybe try some extra kindness next year. But don't worry — Mr. Castro learned his lesson and plans to return the staplers (and bake new cookies) next season.
